Weapon Enhancement

On this server a weapon can change completely once you equip the right unit, armor, mag or character. This page lists every enhancement condition and what it does, so you can tell at a glance what a weapon still needs.

Source: Discord #list-of-combo-bonus-buffed-items and #weapons-priority-list, both written by the development team. Values follow the original English notation and are not translated.
The developer marks the original list as still in progress, so entries may be added or adjusted later.

1How to read the notation

The list uses a few shorthands throughout. They are the developer's own wording.

Notation Meaning
Hits A-B-C Hits per attack in a combo. 5-2-4 means the 1st attack hits 5 times, the 2nd 2 times, the 3rd 4 times.
N / H / S Normal attack / Heavy attack / Special attack.
Required [X] What you must have for the buff to apply. It can be equipment, low HP, a character or a class.
Custom Animation A motion reworked for this server that does not exist in the original game.
Combo-unlocked A weapon that could not combo in the original game can now chain attacks.
Speed +15% Attack speed. This stacks with Battle units.

Everything here started with the Ver.0.7.8 major update, which introduced the framework for modifying items in ways that were not possible before.
