3Where to start

High end content is built for players who already have the gear. The development team's own advice is to work up through the easier content first.

Goal Where to go
Photon Drop and Photon Sphere Respective Tomorrow [EP2] · 8-3
Photon Token Walking through Flames (solo) · Harmony of Despair II (multi)
Weapon Crystal Badge Harmony of Despair I [EP1] · VR Test Destiny: Underground [EP2] · VR Test FINAL: Catastrophe [EP2]
First taste of raid mechanics Silent Nightmare [Raid] [EP1] ★7
Materials and level Neo Maximum Attack: Episode IV [EP4] · VR Test Destiny: Seabed [EP2]
High Hit% weapons Any area with an Area% level of 4. See the Server Systems page.

Quoting the development team on Silent Nightmare: the harder content was designed for players who already have the equipment and the practice. If you are not there yet, build up in the lower difficulty content first. It is not a step to skip.
