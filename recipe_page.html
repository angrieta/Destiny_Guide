Item Combinations

The item database lists what a combined item does, but not what it costs. These are the recipes with their full material lists, plus the reverse question: you are holding a material, so what is it for? Every item has to be at max grind before it can be combined.

Source: the material lists published with each item announcement. A few were posted as images with the list cut off, and those are marked in the card.
