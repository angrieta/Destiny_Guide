PSOBB-Tools

I made this tool to smooth out as many of PSOBB's pain points as possible and make the game more enjoyable to play.

psobb-tools is based on the structure of RPAS - a huge thank you to Roland, who has since retired. Your work made this possible.

⬇ Download PSOBB-Tools.exe
📦 Version 1.0 💻 Windows
