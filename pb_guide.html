PB management: time your next invincibility

The goal is not to reach 100 PB as fast as possible. Build PB, hold just below 100, then reach 100 after the current invincibility ends.

01 / Before you start

Check your MAG first

Your MAG must have an invincibility response to the PB-full trigger. Check the MAG's trigger and activation chance in the database. This technique is not a guarantee of permanent invincibility.

Check MAGs and units in the database

PB/Flow: start with spare slots

The database lists +1 PB per 5 seconds per PB/Flow. At low damage, the community recommends multiple units. Keep essential speed and survival units; add PB/Flow in available slots, then remove one if PB fills too early.

02 / The repeatable cycle

Select a step to see the action. This is a teaching diagram, not a live game timer.

Build PB

While invincible, build PB through normal combat and PB/Flow. Start by aiming for 97–99 PB about 5–10 seconds before invincibility ends. Adjust unit count to your damage output; do not assume a fixed combat gain.

03 / Lower PB with a weapon special

Normal attack → Special attack → quick weapon swap

The reported sequence leaves about 63 PB when started from a full gauge. It is a community reference, not a fixed outcome: timing, interruption and combat gains can change the remainder. Practise the sequence in a safe situation first.

04 / When the cycle breaks

100 PB too early
Remove one PB/Flow and try again. Strong gear such as Ignis or a completed Dark Flow setup may need only one PB/Flow; tune it to your combos rather than copying a fixed count.
PB arrives too late
Add a PB/Flow in a spare slot or stop resetting its timer so often. Recheck the 97–99 PB checkpoint before trying to shorten the gap.
100 PB, but no invincibility
Check the MAG's trigger and chance. Community reports warn that a hit at the invincibility-end frame or reaching 100 at that same moment can miss the new trigger. Do not assume the gauge alone proves protection.
100 PB and below 10% HP together
VEL(JP) reports that the game prioritizes the low-HP trigger when both conditions happen together. Its response or activation chance may differ from PB-full. Avoid relying on this overlap for invincibility.
A hit interrupts the spending animation
An interruption may permit an earlier swap and leave roughly 70–77 PB, according to the discussion. This also means you were exposed to damage: it is not a safe PB-control technique to aim for.
Advanced / area-warp PB spending

VEL(JP) reports that using the special during an area warp can limit spending to about 5–6 PB. A boss teleporter version is much harder. Timing is not documented precisely here: treat it as an optional experiment after you can perform the normal cycle, not a beginner requirement.
