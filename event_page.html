Events

Destiny runs a seasonal event every few weeks, and each one adds items, a limited quest, or a shop trade that disappears when it ends. This page collects what each event contained, when it ran, and how its rewards were obtained.

Source: the event announcement channels on the Destiny Discord, posted by GM VEL(JP). Drop rates are the values announced at the time and may have been adjusted since.

The yearly cycle

Events repeat on a rough annual pattern, so you can plan around them. Dates shift year to year, and revival runs are sometimes slotted between the regular ones.

  • 12 XMAS
  • 02 Valentine

2026.04.29 ~ 2026.05.08 Ended

Summary

  • A revival run held between the Easter event and the 8th Anniversary event.
  • It started after a server restart and ran until the 8th Anniversary event began.
  • Contents are the same as the 2025 run — everything below is from that event, which is what the revival announcement points to.

  • A bank was added to Path to Salvation [Halloween] and the laser fence in Ruins 1 was removed.

Destiny XMAS Event 2025

2025.12.02 ~ 2026.01.01 (4 weeks) Ended

Server-wide boosts

  • ×10 XP and free Section ID changes, for the entire event.
  • Parasitic Gene ‘Flow’ dropped at an improved rate of 1/146.

Photon Tokens and Presents

  • Every monster in Ragol had a chance to drop Christmas Presents and Photon Tokens.
  • Photon Tokens — used to add hit to weapons by speaking to Paganini in the Phantastic Bazaar quest.
  • Christmas Presents — could contain limited edition Xmas apparel, certain Type Weapons with a special, or a Yasminkov 9000m with a special.

Limited items

Christmas Spirit
Xmas limited
  • Launches a volley of 3 candy canes per shot at a single target.
  • Spirit special — hits moving and flying targets without missing.
  • Equippable by Hunter, Ranger and Force.
