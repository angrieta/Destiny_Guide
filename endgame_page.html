How to read this

  • The same quest name can appear twice — an event version is usually rated higher than the year-round one. Walking through Flame is ★5 normally and ★7 in its Halloween version, and Gal Da Val’s Darkness goes from ★3 to ★7 at Easter.
  • Event rows are not playable right now — they only open while that event runs. The Events page has the periods and rewards.
  • Raid rows need a full party — and most of them award a Discord role for clearing under a condition.

Enemy and item colours

Higher-rated quests use recoloured enemies, and the colour tells you what will not work before you waste a special.

Colour What it means
Purple enemy name Immune to Demon’s and Hell. Some of these also have higher HP than normal.
Red enemy name Immune to Demon’s and Hell, and on top of that either much higher HP or a damage-cut barrier.
Flashing item description A particularly rare item, unless it is a tool type.
Yellow item description A custom skinned item.
