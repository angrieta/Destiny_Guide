Shops & Currency

Almost every strong item on this server is traded, not dropped. This page puts the currencies and the trade NPCs side by side, so you can see what a material is for before you start farming it.

Source: the Discord patch note channels and the Bazaar and currency channel. Item names, amounts and drop rates follow the original English notation and are not translated.
