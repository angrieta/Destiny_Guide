Server Systems

Destiny changes a number of things the original Blue Burst never had. This page collects those changes, and the area table that decides which percentages a weapon can roll.

Source: the Discord patch note channels, and the area table posted by the development team on 19 July 2026.

1What is different from the original game

If you are returning from another server, these are the changes you will notice first.

Client settings worth changing

  • Set message speed to 'fastest'. Boss skill names scroll past before the attack lands, and the faster setting gives you more time to react. Recommended for every raid.
  • The Starlight Tower, Distorted Nightmare and The Manipulated Tower require the widescreen client (psobbw.exe). A 4:3 client cannot run them.
  • If area-of-effect markers are hard to see, use the Effect Pack linked in the Discord guides channel.

2Enemy name colours

Instant-kill specials do not work on everything. The name colour tells you before you waste a combo.

Colour Meaning
Default Demon's and Hell work normally.
Purple Immune to Demon's and Hell. Some of these enemies also have more than 32,767 HP.
Red Immune to Demon's and Hell. On a boss it also means very high HP or a damage reduction barrier.

Using Demon's on an immune enemy shows the text IMMUNE in purple, so you can confirm it in the middle of a fight.

3Area% - where high percentages come from

A weapon's attribute and Hit percentage are decided by the area it drops in, not by the monster. Every area carries three Area% levels, and each level caps how high a roll can go.

Area% level Range you can roll
0 −5% ~ 60%
1 −5% ~ 65%
2 −5% ~ 75%
3 −5% ~ 85%
4 −5% ~ 100%

So a 100% Hit weapon can only come from an area that has a level 4 among its three values. Ruins 3, Seabed Lower - Tower, Sub Desert 3, Saint Million and the EP4 boss map are all 4/4/4.
