Useful in-game commands

/bank: bank access · /roominfo: room information · /hh: check Happy Hour · /daily: daily status. Command availability can depend on location and state. Since 0.945, /lobby also works outside quests and HP Material reset is supported; confirm reset options in the game before using them.

Why can’t I add Hit to my Excalibur or TJS?

A September 8 support answer explains that kill-count data occupies an attribute slot. A Lame d’Argent unsealed with two existing attributes can leave Excalibur unable to take another Hit attribute. Check the original weapon’s attributes before unsealing; Sealed J-Sword has a related constraint. Ask support to inspect your item rather than assuming a reset or refund.

Why does my MAG sometimes fail to trigger at 100 PB?

When 100 PB and low HP occur together, the low-HP trigger can take priority. Its activation chance may differ from the PB trigger. Do not assume that reaching 100 PB guarantees invincibility; check the MAG’s conditions and the PB guide.

Why can’t I find a quest reward in the drop table?

The regular drop table and quest-specific reward rules are separate. For example, Soul Eraser’s World of Illusion Olga Flow reward is 1/73 on all IDs. Use the item guide’s acquisition section and its official source for special quest rewards.

Terms you will see often

DAR: chance an enemy drops an item. RDR: rare-drop roll. HH: Happy Hour. PB: Photon Blast. DMC: Damage Cancel. MPC: Millennium Photon Core. A published recipe does not by itself mean the item has been released.
