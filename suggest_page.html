How this works

  • Write in your own language — every post is machine-translated into the site's five languages when you post it. Translations are labelled, and one click shows what the author actually wrote.
  • Back the ones you want — the count is what gets looked at first. One per person per post, and pressing again takes it back.
  • The password is the only key — there is no account and no recovery. Make one up for this page; never reuse a password that matters. It is stored as a one-way hash, never as text.
  • Staff mark what they decided — open, planned, done or declined, with a line saying why. A declined idea stays up rather than quietly disappearing.
