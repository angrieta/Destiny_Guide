Ver.0.945 — the September 1 update

2026.09.01

The official patch note was published on 2026-08-30 and went live after the September 1 server restart.

DROP RATE

The party DAR penalty is eased

DAR — the chance anything drops at all — used to fall sharply as the party grew. That cut is now much smaller: at four players it goes from 60% → 80%, so hunting in a group costs far less than it did.

9th anniversary new item

Event starts 2026.09.18
9TH ANNIVERSARY

AEGIS OF ISOLATION

“A cursed rare shield that isolates its wearer. It disables healing from allies, but in exchange it maximises the effect of every recovery item used by the bearer.”

  • Shield · Class ALL · requires level 150.
  • DFP 120–170 · EVP 180–230 · Resists 32-32-32-24-24 · ATP +60.
  • Healing from other party members is blocked. The wearer can still heal other players and can be revived.
  • Monomate and Dimate heal as much as a Trimate.
  • After the event it is planned to enter normal quests too, but at a lower drop rate.

The full stats were confirmed on September 3. When another player tries to heal the wearer, a red “+0” is displayed.

In development

The Eternal Dimension

Progress update · 2026.09.04
QUEST PREVIEW

THE ETERNAL DIMENSION

“A realm beyond The Phantasmal Dimension, where time loops endlessly and only the strongest return.”

  • EP2 > Special · difficulty 10 · a one-room arena with many waves.
  • A new currency is planned and will be used to obtain a new item.
  • Clearing is optional unless you are pursuing the special role, like The Phantasmal Dimension.
  • Main development is planned after VR:OMEGA.

As of September 4, the barebones quest and several future items are complete. The blue armor shown in announcements is one of those items; its name and stats have not been revealed.
