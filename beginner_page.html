Beginner's Guide

Important Information

If you’re new to the game, the Force class is the easiest and most beginner-friendly choice.

Because Force techniques deal fixed damage, while other classes depend on stats and gear, making them much harder to play on Ultimate difficulty without proper equipment.

Additionally, Force is recommended because it makes farming essential early-game items much easier.

Try asking on Discord if anyone can donate disks - you might be able to get some Tech Disks that way.

Suggested starting paths

Pick the route for your class, then use the detailed Force walkthrough below for the shared setup, Meseta, MAG, and farming steps.

HU Hunter

  1. Start with a Charge Handgun for safe leveling, then add Charge Mechguns when your ATA is reliable.
  2. Carry a Partisan or Sword for groups and a fast single-target weapon for bosses.
  3. Use traps on androids; human Hunters can use support techniques and a ranged weapon to control risky approaches.
  4. Ask for low-level event gear such as Hallowed Garment or Deal With It, which is often inexpensive or donated.
  5. A Hit Charge Calibur is a cheap baseline. While leveling, Vjaya, Yunchang, Kitetsu, and Sword of Despair are practical upgrades that do not require a large detour from EXP farming. Vjaya can be hunted from Hard Bluefull Episode 2 Hildebear in Dolmolm Research.
  6. Treat Berserk weapons such as Yunchang or Zanba as optional until you are comfortable managing HP. A safer weapon you can use consistently is better for a new player.
  7. Plan Twin Cyclone and Rathalos Greatsword as later upgrades, not as requirements before you can begin playing Hunter.

Goal: keep a ranged option, an area weapon, and a bossing weapon ready instead of relying on one melee weapon.

RA Ranger

  1. Use free Meseta on Charge Mechguns or Charge Vulcans with Hit for early single-target and boss damage.
  2. Prioritize Tormentor into Grave Digger, Frozen Shooter, and a Charge Arms or Charge Vulcans. These cover Demon's, freezing, groups, and boss damage.
  3. Use a rifle for long range and a handgun or mechgun for short-range cleanup.
  4. Add early ATA with Ranger Wall or the event shield Deal With It when available.
  5. For solo leveling, a Type Shot with Hell is strong in Hard-Very Hard MAS2. Divine Punishment can speed up MAS4 while its Beat Time effect is active.
  6. Grave Digger can be farmed from the first two rooms of Episode 4 Mop Up Operation 3. Avoid difficult Max Attack S Episode 2 runs until your survival is reliable.
  7. Rainbow Shield and Blue Phantom Shield are accessible defensive upgrades for both Ranger and Hunter; the latter can be farmed safely from Zol Gibbon in the first room of Episode 2 Maximum Attack 4th Stage 2A/B/C.

Goal: cover crowd control, groups, bosses, and both long- and short-range fights before chasing endgame Ranger gear.

FO Force

  1. Collect donated or farmed Tech Disks and set up quickslots first.
  2. Build the 200 MIND MAG and obtain status-immunity units.
  3. Farm high-level techniques, Psycho Wand or Glide Divine, Three Seals, and V802 in the order shown below.
  4. Use fixed technique damage to farm the early gear needed by your other characters.
  5. Mother Garb+ is a useful pre-200 frame when Fluids or TP consumption are inconvenient; it reduces technique TP cost by 50%. Greenill can hunt it from Very Hard Ill Gill, including the early waves of Defend the Main Room.
  6. At level 200, move toward Primal Nexus and V802 instead of overinvesting in temporary leveling armor.
  7. Use Force's safer early farming to collect the weapons and shields your future Hunter or Ranger will need.

Goal: establish a low-cost farming character that can fund and equip future Ranger or Hunter builds.

Budget tip: Non-rare Charge Rayguns, Vulcans, and Arms are available in the Phantastic Bazaar for 1 PD each, while the server's free Meseta makes Charge specials practical for everyday play.

Leveling tip: Once Central Control Area is unlocked, repeatedly defeat Gal Gryphon and return to the lobby for one of the fastest leveling routes. Unlock CCA once per character by activating the switches at the end of Seaside, Jungle, and Mountain. In Multiplayer Mode, the room leader must press each switch; an unlock completed in Solo Mode also carries over.

10 Nuke Force completed ✨

Now you can start hunting for the gear you want.

Tip 1: If you keep an eye on Discord Trades and Tradelists, you can often find items like Claw of Elements for 10 PD and Adept for 8 PD at a cheap price.

Tip 2: Get Skyfall. It will definitely help. You can usually buy it for around 20-30 PD.

Tip 3: When it comes to weapons, HIT is the top priority. 1 HIT = 1 ATA, so higher HIT directly means better accuracy.

The HIT stat can be increased up to 80. You can raise it using Donate Tickets or Photon Tickets.
